Simple Ripple Animation for Flutter

A lightweight, high-performance Flutter package for creating concentric circular ripple animations, pulsing avatar glows, radar beacons, and call-ringing waves across Android, iOS, Web, macOS, Windows, and Linux.

Interactive Playground & Customization Features

Test ripple animation parameters live in your browser: minimum radius, maximum radius, wave count, duration in milliseconds, delay, repeat pulse, and custom color palettes.

  • Avatar Profile Pulse Effect
  • Incoming Phone Call Ringing Waves
  • Voice Assistant Microphone Pulse
  • Radar and Sonar Beacon Scanner
  • Notification Alert Pulse Badge
  • Audio and Music Visualizer Button
